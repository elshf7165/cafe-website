Welcome to Coffee Feans!

This is a Black-owned café founded by Fatima Elsheikh and her family. Built on a love for coffee and community, our space is all about feeling comfortable, welcomed, and at ease. Whether you're grabbing a quick drink, relaxing in our lounge, getting work done, or spending time with friends, there’s a spot here for you. From cozy seating and quiet corners to games and shared spaces, everything is designed to fit your mood.


Good coffee, warm energy, and a place that feels like yours.
